RE 跨国数据库：可检索、可排序、可对比

RE Cross-Country Data Explorer: Searchable, Sortable, Comparable

本页收录 15 个示意性国家案例在 2019、2021、2023 三个年份的面板数据，涵盖 V（反应弹性）、C（转化率）、F（制度摩擦）及 RE 综合得分，并附带四个原始可观察维度指标。您可以按年份切换截面、搜索、排序、按区域筛选、导出 CSV，并选取多个国家生成对比图表与时间序列趋势图。

This page contains panel data for 15 illustrative country cases across 2019, 2021, and 2023, covering V (Responsive Elasticity), C (Conversion Rate), F (Institutional Friction), and composite RE scores, plus the four raw observable-dimension indicators. Switch year snapshots, search, sort, filter by region, export CSV, and select multiple countries to generate comparison charts and a time-series trend chart.

方法论声明：本数据库中的数值为示意性专家判断估计值，用于演示 RE 框架的操作化逻辑与本网站的数据可视化能力，并非官方统计数据或经过同行评审的实证测量结果。任何实际研究应用须基于第3节所述的多维可观察指标，通过规范的数据收集、编码与信效度检验重新构建。 Methodological Disclaimer: Values in this database are illustrative expert-judgment estimates, intended to demonstrate the RE framework's operationalization logic and this site's data-visualization capability — they are not official statistics or peer-reviewed empirical measurements. Any actual research application should reconstruct these values through rigorous data collection, coding, and validity/reliability testing based on the multidimensional observable indicators described in Section 3.
