资源重配置学派核心术语表

Glossary of Core Terms in the Resource Reallocation School

以下条目提供本理论框架各核心构念的规范定义，供跨国比较研究与同行复制检验时统一参照。

The entries below provide canonical definitions of the framework's core constructs, serving as a shared reference for cross-national comparative research and replication.

RE

资源再配置效率 (Resource Reallocation Efficiency)

Resource Reallocation Efficiency (RE)

一个国家在战略领域内重新分配资源以实现边际效用最大化的经验可测能力。形式化为 RE = VC/(1+F)，是本理论框架的核心因变量/解释变量，取决于反应弹性（V）与转化率（C）之积，并受制度摩擦（F）的折减。

An empirically measurable capacity of a state to reallocate resources within strategic domains so as to maximize marginal utility. Formalized as RE = VC/(1+F), it is the framework's core explanatory construct, determined by the product of Responsive Elasticity (V) and Conversion Rate (C), discounted by Institutional Friction (F).

核心构念Core Construct跨国可比Cross-nationally Comparable

V

反应弹性 (Responsive Elasticity)

Responsive Elasticity (V)

一个国家资源配置对边际效用梯度变化的可衡量响应程度，是 RE 的关键子指标。可观察的参照指标包括：政策调整速度、跨部门资源流动强度、战略重新排序的时间模式。

The measurable degree to which a state's resource allocation responds to shifts in the marginal-utility gradient; a key sub-indicator of RE. Observable reference indicators include policy adjustment speed, cross-sectoral resource flow intensity, and the temporal pattern of strategic reordering.

子指标Sub-Indicator先于绩效测量Measured Prior to Performance

F

制度摩擦 (Institutional Friction)

Institutional Friction (F)

阻碍资源快速再配置的结构性约束，通过监管延迟、官僚僵化指数和制度程序约束来操作化。在 RE 公式中作为分母的折减项（1+F），F 越大，实际重配置效率被压缩得越显著。

The structural constraints that impede rapid resource reallocation, operationalized via regulatory delay, a bureaucratic rigidity index, and institutional procedural constraints. In the RE formula it appears as the denominator's discount term (1+F): larger F more strongly compresses actual reallocation efficiency.

结构性修正因子Structural Correction Factor分母折减项Denominator Discount

C

转化率 (Conversion Rate)

Conversion Rate (C)

资源转化为战略成果的可观察速度和有效性，通过技术吸收能力和产业适应速度的指标来衡量。在 RE 公式中与 V 相乘，共同构成分子部分。

The observable speed and effectiveness with which resources are converted into strategic outcomes, measured through indicators of technology absorption capacity and industrial adaptation speed. In the RE formula it is multiplied by V to form the numerator.

结构性修正因子Structural Correction Factor分子构成项Numerator Component

形式化基础项：边际效用最大化模型

Formal Foundation Terms: The Marginal Utility Maximization Model

以下五个术语构成 RE = VC/(1+F) 之上的微观经济学基础，详见理论全文第 2.5 节。

The following five terms constitute the micro-economic foundation underlying RE = VC/(1+F); see Section 2.5 of the full paper for details.

U

总体效用函数 (Aggregate Utility Function)

Aggregate Utility Function (U)

国家在既定外部战略环境（S）下，将资源配置于全部 n 个战略领域所获得的总体效用，形式化为 U = Σi=1n ui(Ri, S)。它是理解"国家为何以及如何重新配置资源"这一行为动机的顶层构念，RE 描述的是实现该效用最大化过程中的实际效率。

The total utility a state derives from allocating resources across all n strategic domains under a given external strategic environment (S), formalized as U = Σi=1n ui(Ri, S). It is the top-level construct explaining why and how states reallocate resources in the first place; RE describes the realized efficiency of pursuing this maximization.

形式化基础Formal Foundation顶层构念Top-Level Construct

ui

子领域效用函数 (Sub-Domain Utility Function)

Sub-Domain Utility Function (ui)

第 i 个战略领域（如国防、科技、外交、经济）单独贡献给总体效用 U 的函数，其自变量为投入该领域的资源量 Ri 与外部战略环境 S。ui 的具体形式因领域而异，通常假设边际递减，是构造 U 与 MUi 的基础单元。

The function through which the i-th strategic domain (e.g., defense, technology, diplomacy, economy) contributes to aggregate utility U, taking as arguments the resources allocated to that domain (Ri) and the external strategic environment (S). Its precise functional form varies by domain and is typically assumed to exhibit diminishing marginal returns; it is the basic building block from which U and MUi are constructed.

形式化基础Formal Foundation领域层构念Domain-Level Construct

Ri

领域资源配置量 (Resources Allocated to Domain i)

Resources Allocated to Domain i (Ri)

国家实际投入第 i 个战略领域的资源存量或流量（可为财政拨款、人力资本、产业产能等的复合度量）。Ri 的跨期调整速度与幅度正是反应弹性（V）的经验观测对象。

The stock or flow of resources a state actually commits to the i-th strategic domain (a composite measure that may include fiscal outlays, human capital, or industrial capacity). The speed and magnitude of intertemporal adjustment in Ri is precisely what Responsive Elasticity (V) empirically observes.

形式化基础Formal Foundation可观测变量Observable Variable

S

外部战略环境 (External Strategic Environment)

External Strategic Environment (S)

影响各领域效用函数形状的外生状态变量，涵盖地缘政治态势、全球技术竞争格局、联盟结构等国家自身难以短期改变的外部约束。S 的变化正是触发资源重配置的"边际效用梯度"变化的根源。

The exogenous state variable that shapes the form of each domain's utility function, encompassing geopolitical conditions, the global technology-competition landscape, alliance structures, and other external constraints a state cannot easily alter in the short run. Shifts in S are the ultimate source of the changing marginal-utility gradient that triggers resource reallocation.

形式化基础Formal Foundation外生变量Exogenous Variable

MUi

边际效用 (Marginal Utility)

Marginal Utility (MUi)

总体效用 U 对第 i 个领域资源投入 Ri 的偏导数，MUi = ∂U/∂Ri，衡量在其他领域配置不变的条件下，向领域 i 追加一个单位资源所带来的效用增量。理性国家应持续调整 Ri 直至各领域边际效用趋于相等（ΔU/ΔR → 最大化），这正是反应弹性（V）与转化率（C）在微观层面得以实现的机制基础。

The partial derivative of aggregate utility U with respect to resources allocated to domain i, MUi = ∂U/∂Ri, measuring the incremental utility gained from one additional unit of resource committed to domain i, holding allocations elsewhere fixed. A rational state should keep adjusting Ri until marginal utilities equalize across domains (ΔU/ΔR → Maximize) — the micro-level mechanism through which Responsive Elasticity (V) and Conversion Rate (C) are realized.

形式化基础Formal Foundation一阶条件First-Order Condition

财政响应能力 (Fiscal Responsiveness)

Fiscal Responsiveness

RE 的可观察维度之一，衡量国家在预算周期内快速调整拨款、追加战略投入的能力，主要为 V 的经验基础之一。

One of RE's observable dimensions, measuring a state's capacity to rapidly adjust appropriations and inject strategic investment within budget cycles; primarily an empirical basis for V.

可观察维度Observable Dimension

技术转换速度 (Technological Conversion Speed)

Technological Conversion Speed

RE 的可观察维度之一，衡量既有产业与研发能力向战略优先领域转化的速度，主要为 C 的经验基础之一。

One of RE's observable dimensions, measuring the speed at which existing industrial and R&D capacity is redirected toward strategic priorities; primarily an empirical basis for C.

可观察维度Observable Dimension

监管适应性 (Regulatory Adaptability)

Regulatory Adaptability

RE 的可观察维度之一，衡量监管体系为新战略方向让路、修订规则的弹性程度；较高的监管适应性对应较低的制度摩擦（F）。

One of RE's observable dimensions, measuring the elasticity with which regulatory regimes revise rules to accommodate new strategic directions; higher regulatory adaptability corresponds to lower institutional friction (F).

可观察维度Observable Dimension

官僚摩擦 (Bureaucratic Friction)

Bureaucratic Friction

RE 的可观察维度之一，衡量科层制度内部否决点、审批链条对资源流动的阻滞程度；是制度摩擦（F）的核心观测渠道。

One of RE's observable dimensions, measuring the degree to which veto points and approval chains within bureaucracies impede resource flow; a core observational channel for institutional friction (F).

可观察维度Observable Dimension

边际效用梯度 (Marginal Utility Gradient)

Marginal Utility Gradient

指资源在不同战略用途之间的边际效用差异随外部环境变化而产生的变化趋势，是触发反应弹性（V）测量的理论前提。

The trend of change in the marginal-utility differential of resources across strategic uses, driven by shifting external conditions; the theoretical trigger condition for measuring responsive elasticity (V).

触发条件Trigger Condition

反向因果问题 (Reverse Causality)

Reverse Causality

指绩效结果反过来"决定"测量指标的方法论风险。本框架通过将反应弹性与既有结构特征（而非同期绩效）挂钩加以规避。

The methodological risk that performance outcomes retroactively "determine" the measurement indicators. This framework avoids it by anchoring responsive elasticity to pre-existing structural traits rather than contemporaneous performance.

方法论风险Methodological Risk

遗漏变量偏差 (Omitted Variable Bias)

Omitted Variable Bias

指因未纳入相关控制变量而导致因果推断产生偏误的风险。本框架通过控制资源禀赋总量、地缘政治背景和制度基线加以缓解。

The risk of biased causal inference arising from the omission of relevant control variables. This framework mitigates it by controlling for total resource endowments, geopolitical context, and institutional baselines.

方法论风险Methodological Risk
