资源重配置学派与既有国际关系理论的比较

Comparing the Resource Reallocation School with Existing IR Theories

资源重配置学派并非要取代结构现实主义、制度理论或国内政治理论，而是补充其解释盲区：当禀赋、制度形式或偏好相似时，是什么导致了战略结果的分化？答案在于动态调整能力——资源重配置效率（RE）。

The Resource Reallocation School does not seek to replace structural realism, institutionalism, or domestic-politics theory — it complements their explanatory blind spot: when endowments, institutional form, or preferences are similar, what causes strategic outcomes to diverge? The answer lies in dynamic adjustment capacity — Resource Reallocation Efficiency (RE).

资源重配置学派的定位声明

Positioning Statement of the Resource Reallocation School

承认存量的重要性

Acknowledges the Importance of Stocks

RE 框架不否认物质能力分配对战略选项设立了边界条件，而是探讨在既定边界内的调整速度差异。

The RE framework does not deny that material capability distribution sets boundary conditions for strategic options; it examines differential adjustment speed within those boundaries.

将制度内生化而非否定

Endogenizes, Rather Than Dismisses, Institutions

制度依然重要，但被重新表述为可测量的摩擦系数，而非预先决定行为的黑箱。

Institutions still matter, but are reformulated as a measurable friction coefficient rather than a black box that predetermines behavior.

为偏好提供实现条件

Supplies the Realization Condition for Preferences

偏好解释了国家"想要什么"，而 RE 解释了国家能否"及时做到"。

Preferences explain what a state "wants"; RE explains whether a state can achieve it "in time."
