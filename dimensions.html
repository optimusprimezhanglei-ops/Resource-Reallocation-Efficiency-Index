反应弹性、制度摩擦与转化率的可视化拆解

Visualizing Responsive Elasticity, Institutional Friction, and Conversion Rate

本页以图表方式呈现 RE 公式中三个核心结构变量（V、F、C）及其四个可观察维度的构成逻辑，图表数据为示例性质，用于说明测量思路，而非实证结论。

This page visualizes the three core structural variables in the RE formula (V, F, C) and their four observable dimensions. Chart data is illustrative — intended to demonstrate measurement logic, not to assert empirical findings.

四个可观察维度 Four Observable Dimensions

RE 的构成渠道

Constituent Channels of RE

01

财政响应能力

Fiscal Responsiveness

对应 VMaps to V

衡量国家在预算周期内快速调整拨款、追加战略投入的能力。

Measures the capacity to rapidly reallocate appropriations and inject strategic investment within budget cycles.

  • 追加预算审批平均耗时
  • Average approval time for supplementary budgets
  • 跨部门财政转移规模
  • Scale of inter-sectoral fiscal transfers
  • 应急拨款机制的可用性
  • Availability of emergency appropriation mechanisms
02

技术转换速度

Technological Conversion Speed

对应 CMaps to C

衡量既有产业与研发能力向战略优先领域转化的速度。

Measures the speed at which existing industrial and R&D capacity is redirected toward strategic priorities.

  • 技术吸收周期（引进到规模化应用）
  • Technology absorption cycle (introduction to scaled use)
  • 产业链重组速度
  • Speed of supply-chain reconfiguration
  • 研发资源再分配弹性
  • Elasticity of R&D resource reallocation
03

监管适应性

Regulatory Adaptability

对应 F（低摩擦方向）Maps to F (low-friction direction)

衡量监管体系为新战略方向让路、修订规则的弹性程度。

Measures the elasticity of regulatory regimes in revising rules to accommodate new strategic directions.

  • 法规修订平均周期
  • Average cycle for rule revision
  • 豁免与快速通道机制的存在
  • Existence of exemption / fast-track mechanisms
  • 跨监管机构协调成本
  • Cross-agency regulatory coordination cost
04

官僚摩擦

Bureaucratic Friction

对应 F（高摩擦方向）Maps to F (high-friction direction)

衡量科层制度内部否决点、审批链条对资源流动的阻滞程度。

Measures the degree to which veto points and approval chains within bureaucracies impede resource flow.

  • 否决点数量
  • Number of veto points
  • 审批链条平均层级
  • Average number of approval-chain tiers
  • 程序性听证/复议耗时
  • Time consumed by procedural hearings / review

Illustrative Cross-Country Charts

The following charts use hypothetical illustrative data (not actual statistics) to demonstrate how the four dimensions can be transformed into comparable visualizations.

四维度雷达图：三个假设性国家案例

Radar Chart: Three Hypothetical Country Cases

雷达图展示假设国家 A、B、C 在四个可观察维度上的相对得分（0–10 标度），用于说明多维指标如何共同刻画 RE 的结构画像。

The radar chart shows hypothetical countries A, B, and C scored (0–10 scale) across the four observable dimensions, illustrating how multidimensional indicators jointly depict a state's RE profile.

制度摩擦（F）与转化率（C）的权衡关系

Trade-off Between Institutional Friction (F) and Conversion Rate (C)

散点图展示假设样本国家的 F 值与 C 值分布，气泡大小代表反应弹性 V。位于右上方（低F、高C、大气泡）的国家理论上具备更高的 RE。

The scatter plot shows hypothetical countries' F and C values; bubble size represents V. Countries in the upper-right (low F, high C, large bubble) theoretically exhibit higher RE.

Composition of Responsive Elasticity (V) Sub-Indicators

RE 结构分解：从公式到结果

RE Structural Decomposition: From Formula to Outcome

环形图示意在给定 V×C 情形下，制度摩擦（F）折减效应所占的相对比例，直观呈现"摩擦吞噬效率"的机制。

The doughnut chart illustrates, for a given V×C, the relative share consumed by the friction (F) discount effect — visualizing the mechanism by which "friction erodes efficiency."
