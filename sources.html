如何用真实统计数据构建 RE 指标

Constructing RE Indicators From Real-World Statistical Data

The values in this site's database are illustrative estimates. Researchers wishing to build a publishable, replicable empirical dataset may use the table below to map the four observable dimensions onto established authoritative international data sources. This page does not host downloaded raw data — it provides methodological mapping guidance and retrieval pathways only.

构建流程建议 Suggested Construction Workflow

从原始数据到 RE 指数的五步流程

A Five-Step Workflow From Raw Data to the RE Index

① 数据采集

① Data Collection

从上表候选数据源下载覆盖目标国家与年份的原始指标面板数据。

Download raw panel data covering target countries and years from the candidate sources above.

② 标准化

② Standardization

对量纲不一致的原始指标进行 Min-Max 或 Z-score 标准化，统一映射至 0–10 标度。

Apply Min-Max or Z-score standardization to indicators with inconsistent units, mapping them onto a common 0–10 scale.

③ 权重确定

③ Weight Elicitation

通过主成分分析（PCA）、专家德尔菲法或结构方程模型确定各维度到 V/C/F 的聚合权重。

Use Principal Component Analysis (PCA), expert Delphi methods, or Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) to determine dimension-to-V/C/F aggregation weights.

④ 指数合成

④ Index Synthesis

代入 RE = VC/(1+F) 计算复合指数，并进行跨国、跨年份的稳健性检验。

Substitute into RE = VC/(1+F) to compute the composite index, then conduct cross-country and cross-year robustness checks.

⑤ 效度与信度检验

⑤ Validity & Reliability Testing

通过 Cronbach's α 检验内部一致性，通过与既有相关构念（如国家能力指数、政府效能指数）的相关分析检验构念效度，并使用 6 节所述策略排除反向因果与遗漏变量偏差。

Test internal consistency via Cronbach's α, assess construct validity through correlation analysis with related established constructs (e.g., State Capacity Index, Government Effectiveness Index), and apply the strategies described in Section 6 to rule out reverse causality and omitted variable bias.
